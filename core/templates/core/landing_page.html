Frequently Asked Questions

What is Competitive Programming?

Instead of building functional projects or apps, you solve problems. It requires logical, algorithmic, and sometimes mathematical thinking, but Algostep will help you with that. The goal is to not only solve problems, but solve them fast and efficiently.

Where can I practice Competitive Programming problems?

There are various websites to practice, but the main ones are Codeforces, LeetCode, HackerRank, AtCoder, and the official USA Computing Olympiad (USACO). Every source is different, and you are not limited to just these websites.

What language is best for Competitive Programming?

This is entirely up to your preference, as many programming languages can be used for Competitive Programming. However, the most prominent three languages that are used are C++, Java, and Python. Out of the three, C++ is the most widely used, for its incredibly fast execution speed.
