Pattern Time Complexity Space Complexity Best Use Cases
Opposite Direction
(Left & Right)		 O(n) O(1) Finding pairs; processing elements from both ends
Same Direction
(Fast & Slow) 		O(n) O(1) In-place modifying; detecting cycles; finding midpoints
Sliding Window
(Dynamic or Fixed) 		O(n) O(1) or O(k)* Tracking continguous subarrays; finding subarrays/substrings that match a condition

*O(k) space complexity if you are tracking elements and are storing them in a data structure.
